Before you send your first note

Does the other person need the app?

Yes. Both creating and revealing message videos use Secret Video Message on iPhone, iPad, or Mac. The recipient saves the file and imports it into the app.

Is this encrypted messaging?

No. Text is encoded in QR frames inside a video. It is a playful way to reveal a note, not a secure channel for passwords, financial details, or other sensitive information.

Can I send it through a chat app?

Send the original video file, ideally as a file or document. Chat apps may compress videos, and trimming, filters, or compression can make the message unreadable.
