Private video message app for hidden notes

Secret Video Message is a playful way to send a note as a video, then reveal the hidden text later in the app.

Ideas for hidden message videos

Surprise notesSend a birthday line, a tiny celebration, or a quiet reveal.
Private cluesShare a hint, event code, or puzzle clue that is meant to be opened later.
Personal check-insMake a private note feel more intentional than a plain text message.

Not a heavy security tool

Secret Video Message is designed for playful privacy and reveal-later moments. It should not be treated as a replacement for secure messaging or long-term secret storage.
