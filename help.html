Secret Video Message Help

What it does

Secret Video Message lets you hide a message in a video, send a private video message, and reveal the hidden message later.

How to hide a message in a video

1. Open Hide a Message.

2. Type the note you want to hide.

3. Choose a scene style if you like.

4. Tap the button to generate the message video.

5. Save or share the exported video.

How to reveal a hidden message

1. Open Reveal a Message.

2. Choose a message video from Photos or Files.

3. Wait a moment while the app reads the hidden content.

4. View or copy the recovered text.

How to use the Share Extension

1. Open a message video in another app, such as Photos or Files.

2. Tap the system Share button.

3. Find and tap Reveal Message in the share sheet.

4. The hidden text will open in the extension and can be copied there.

Troubleshooting Secret Video Message

The app cannot read my video.
Make sure you selected a video created by Secret Video Message, then try again with the original exported file.

I cannot find the Share Extension.
Open the share sheet, scroll through the app list, and make sure the extension is enabled on your device.

The recovered message looks incomplete.
Try the original video again instead of a recompressed copy from another service or app.
