Secret Video Message Privacy Policy

Effective date: Apr 15, 2026

Overview

Secret Video Message is designed to work with as little data collection as possible.

In normal use, your message content, generated videos, and related settings stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

What we collect

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or profile information to use the app.

We do not use third-party analytics to track your activity inside the app.

How data is handled

The app may store local settings and files needed to create or reveal message videos on your device.

If you download or purchase the app through Apple platforms, Apple may process platform-level billing or delivery under its own policies.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, the effective date on this page will be updated.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact cameoshell09@gmail.com.
