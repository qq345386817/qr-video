How to reveal a hidden message from a video

If someone sends you a Secret Video Message, use the app to import the video and bring the hidden note back into view.

Steps

  1. Open Secret Video Message.
  2. Choose Reveal a Message.
  3. Select the message video from Photos or Files.
  4. Wait while the app reads the video frames.
  5. Copy or save the recovered text when it appears.

Use the Share Extension

You can also open a message video from another app, tap the system Share button, and choose Reveal Message to recover the text without manually opening the main app first.

If the video cannot be read

Try the original exported file. A copied or recompressed video may lose details needed to recover the hidden message.
