Secret Video Message Support

Need help with a hidden message video, the Share Extension, or App Store download? Start here.

Contact

Email support at cameoshell09@gmail.com. Include your device, system version, and whether you are trying to hide or reveal a message.

Useful links

Help guide Step-by-step help for creating and revealing message videos. Privacy Policy How local videos, hidden messages, and app data are handled. App Store Download Secret Video Message for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Common support topics

  • Use the original exported video when revealing a hidden message.
  • Avoid sending through services that heavily recompress video before decoding.
  • Enable the Share Extension from the system share sheet if it does not appear immediately.
